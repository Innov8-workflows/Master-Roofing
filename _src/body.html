Real jobs, as they happen

Photographed on site, so you can see how a job is set up before the roof work starts.

Project 01

Scaffolded on a terraced street

A terraced house gives you no side access and no room for mess. The scaffold went up across the front in two boarded lifts with a ladder to the roof, and the pavement below was coned and taped off so passers-by stayed clear of the work.
